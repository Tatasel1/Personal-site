I am now a student at Universitatea Nationala de Stiinte si Tehnologie Politehnica Bucuresti, pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering. Here, I'm learning more than just how to code; I'm learning how to think like an engineer. From the complexities of data structures and algorithms to low-level systems , I'm building my toolkit to create technical solutions that are scalable, efficient, and robust.

My Approach

I see engineering as a blend of two things: knowing the right tools and knowing how to work with people.

On the technical side, I'm building a strong foundation. I'm most comfortable with languages like C, C++, and C#, but I also have experience with the versatility of Python and web fundamentals like HTML/CSS. I've spent plenty of time working with IDE's like Visual Studio Code, CLion, and IntelliJ, and I'm comfortable navigating both Windows and Linux.

But the tools are only half the story. My experience in volunteer organizations like LSAC and Interact taught me that the most complex problems are solved by people, not just code. This is where I've developed my skills in teamwork, clear communication, and patience. I'm an organized person who understands how to manage time and meet deadlines, but I also bring a sense of responsibility and initiative to a team. I'm always looking for a more innovative way to approach a problem and am not afraid to take the first step.
